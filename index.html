What we're building

From timber to transport.

NBD Lumber aims to build a business around processing wood products and moving them efficiently to customers.

Funds are intended to support machinery and equipment, wood processing operations, trucks, and transportation needs.

This is a proposed business fundraising campaign. Plans and allocation details will be updated as the project develops.

The plan

01 / EQUIPMENT

Build capacity

Work toward acquiring suitable equipment for timber and wood processing.

02 / OPERATIONS

Process wood

Develop a practical operation for producing and handling wood products.

03 / TRANSPORT

Move products

Plan for trucks and transportation to deliver materials to customers.
