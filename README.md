🌲 NBD Lumber

Building the Future of Lumber and Wood Processing

Welcome to the official NBD Lumber Fundraising Project!

NBD Lumber is a developing business project focused on timber processing, lumber production, wood products, and transportation.

Our mission is to build a successful lumber business by investing in equipment, trucks, machinery, and infrastructure.

💰 Fundraising Goal: $300,000

We are launching a worldwide fundraising campaign with an ambitious goal:

Raise $300,000 through voluntary contributions.

Our idea is simple:

If 300,000 people or companies each contribute just $1, we can reach our goal!

Every contribution, regardless of size, would help us move closer to building our business.

🚛 What Will the Money Support?

Funds raised are intended to support:

* 🌲 Timber and wood-processing operations
* 🪵 Lumber production and equipment
* 🚛 Trucks and transportation
* ⚙️ Machinery and business infrastructure
* 🏗️ Business development and operational expenses

🌎 Worldwide Support

We welcome support from individuals, businesses, and organizations worldwide, where permitted.

Our campaign encourages voluntary contributions starting at $1.
